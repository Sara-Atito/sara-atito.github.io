## Research

- **Visual foundation models:** Self-supervised learning, masked image modelling and concept-aware representations that reduce reliance on large labelled datasets.
- **Multimodal understanding:** Connecting vision, audio and language through efficient adaptation, information flow and interpretable multimodal representations.
- **AI for health and science:** Transferable visual representations for medical, microscopy and multi-channel images, with a focus on robustness across datasets.
