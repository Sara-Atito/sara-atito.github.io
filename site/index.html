01 / ABOUT

Building understanding.
Sharing what we learn.

I am a Lecturer at the University of Surrey, jointly appointed in the Centre for Vision, Speech and Signal Processing (CVSSP) and the Surrey Institute for People-Centred Artificial Intelligence (PAI). Previously, I was a Surrey Future Fellow (2023–2026) and a Research Fellow (2021–2023).

My research focuses on self-supervised learning, visual foundation models and multimodal AI. I am particularly interested in how models discover meaningful concepts, connect information across modalities, and transfer to new tasks efficiently. I also work on applications in healthcare and scientific imaging.
